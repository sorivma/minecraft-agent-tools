# Community conduct

Use this project to help people understand and improve their Minecraft worlds.
Be respectful, patient and specific. English and Russian contributions are welcome.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, deliberate exposure of private
information and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Critique code and
ideas rather than people. Respect other players' privacy in logs and screenshots.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation
when necessary. To report a concern without exposing private details, contact
[@sorivma](https://github.com/sorivma) through a private channel; if no private
channel is available, open an issue requesting one without naming other people
or posting sensitive information. Security problems belong in the private
[security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
